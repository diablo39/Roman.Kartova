# Webhook integrations — Outbound: signing what we send

Section of `knowledge/security/webhook-integrations.md`.


Everything above, mirrored, because our consumers deserve what we demand from providers: an
HMAC-SHA256 (or asymmetric) signature over delivery-ID, timestamp, and raw body in documented
headers; per-endpoint signing secrets generated by us (CSPRNG, SEC-033) and shown once;
rotation support with an overlap window; timestamps from NTP-disciplined clocks; retries with
backoff that re-sign with a fresh timestamp and reuse the same delivery ID, so consumer-side
idempotency works. Document the verification recipe for consumers — the scheme is only a
control when the receiver can implement it without guessing.
